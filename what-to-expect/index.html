Your visit, step by step

Before, during, and after.

Here is what we ask before you come in, what to plan for, and how to care for the IV site after.

  1. 01

    Before your visit

    • Eat a snack beforehand.
    • Drink water ahead of time.
    • Wear loose, comfortable clothing with easy sleeve access.
    • Avoid antihistamines and decongestants beforehand.
  2. 02

    During your visit

    Plan on about 30 to 45 minutes.

    Thrive IV Solutions is physician-supervised, overseen by Dr. R. Brookshire, a board-certified vascular surgeon.

  3. 03

    After your visit

    • Wait 1 hour before getting the IV site wet.
    • Wait 24 hours before strenuous activity.
    • Keep hydrating.
    • Avoid rough contact with the site for 48 hours.

    IV therapy supplements a healthy lifestyle. It does not replace one.

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

How long does a session take?

Most IV sessions take about 30 to 45 minutes.

What should I do before my visit?

Eat a snack, drink water ahead of time, and wear loose, comfortable clothing with easy sleeve access. Avoid antihistamines and decongestants beforehand.

What should I do after my visit?

Wait 1 hour before getting the IV site wet and 24 hours before strenuous activity. Keep hydrating, and avoid rough contact with the site for 48 hours.

Who oversees the IV therapy?

Thrive IV Solutions is a physician-supervised clinic. It is overseen by Dr. R. Brookshire, a board-certified vascular surgeon.
