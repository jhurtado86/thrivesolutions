How mobile visits work

Four steps, from your call to your session.

  1. 01

    Call us

    Tell us where you are and which drips you want. (956) 322-7662, Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM.

  2. 02

    We confirm the details

    Your location, your drips, and the time. If you are more than 30 miles from our Edinburg office, we go over the travel details on the call.

  3. 03

    We arrive and set up

    Prep is the same as a clinic visit: eat a snack, drink water ahead of time, and wear loose clothing with easy sleeve access.

  4. 04

    Your session

    Plan on about 30 to 45 minutes. Aftercare is the same as after a clinic visit.

FAQ

Mobile visits, answered.

Where do you offer mobile IV therapy?

At homes, offices, and events across the Rio Grande Valley. Our office is at 2511 Cornerstone Blvd in Edinburg, TX.

Is there a travel fee?

Yes. The travel fee is $20 within 30 miles of our Edinburg office. Beyond 30 miles, call for details.

Are the drips and prices the same as the clinic?

Yes. Mobile visits use the same IV and wellness menu and the same prices as our Edinburg clinic. Every price is in whole dollars.

Is mobile IV therapy physician-supervised?

Yes. Thrive IV Solutions is physician-supervised, in the clinic and on mobile visits. It is overseen by Dr. R. Brookshire, a board-certified vascular surgeon.

How do I book a mobile visit?

Call (956) 322-7662. Mobile visits are available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM.

Can you come to an event or a group?

Yes. Event and group bookings are planned by phone. Call us and we will set it up with you.

How long does a mobile session take?

About 30 to 45 minutes, the same as a clinic session.
