Why it matters

Why physician supervision matters.

An IV drip puts fluids straight into a vein. That is a medical procedure, and it deserves a physician's oversight. Thrive IV Solutions is a physician-supervised clinic in Edinburg, TX.

The physician is Dr. R. Brookshire, a board-certified vascular surgeon. Vascular surgery is the specialty that focuses on blood vessels, including the veins an IV uses.

What to expect

Plan on 30 to 45 minutes.

That is about how long a session takes. A little prep before you come in makes the visit easier.

Before your visit

  • Eat a snack.
  • Drink water ahead of time.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing with easy sleeve access.
  • Skip antihistamines and decongestants beforehand.
